## 2. The essentials

Kinderwelt processes progress, settings and optional character images exclusively on the device and does not transmit this content to us or third parties. The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking tools and requests no Android permissions. Cloud backups of app data are disabled.

## 3. What is saved on the device

To make the game work, the app saves progress, points and rewards; settings such as language, selected character and world; and, optionally, a custom character image (see section 4) in its private storage on the device.

Other apps have no access to this storage. The data is not transmitted or included in app backups. We as the provider have no access to it.

## 4. Custom character image

Parents can select their own photo as a character in the parents area. Selection uses the operating system's image selector. The app receives only the chosen image and needs no permission for this.

A preview is prepared after selection. Only after confirmation is a reduced copy, stripped of metadata such as location and camera information, saved. The original file remains unchanged in its existing location.

The copy is saved only in private app storage. It is not uploaded, shared or included in app backups.

## 5. Children

Kinderwelt is aimed at primary-school children. Progress, settings and character images are processed exclusively on the device and are not transmitted to us or third parties. Photo selection is in the parents area.

## 6. Deleting data

The character image can be removed at any time in the parents area. Resetting progress in that area deletes the character image and returns progress and settings to their defaults. Uninstalling causes Android to remove all private app data from the device.

This does not affect the originally selected photo. It remains in your gallery or its original location and is never changed or deleted by Kinderwelt.

## 7. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. When downloading, installing and updating it, Google processes data under its own responsibility, such as information about your Google account and device. Depending on device settings, Google may also collect technical diagnostic data such as crash reports and make aggregated information available to us. Kinderwelt itself contains no diagnostic or crash-reporting tools. See Google's privacy policy for details.

## 8. Contact by email

If you email us, for example with feedback or an error report, we use your email address and message only to handle your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not disclose this data and delete it once the request is resolved unless statutory retention requirements prevent this. Please do not send photos of children; such emails are not considered and are immediately and permanently deleted.

## 9. Your rights

Where we process your personal data, meaning when you contact us by email, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Articles 15–21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

## 10. Changes

If the app changes how it processes data, we update this privacy policy. The current version is always available at this address.
