## Offline play

Kinderwelt needs no account. Tasks, pictures, speech and music are bundled. The app contains no advertising, purchases, analytics, telemetry, camera/microphone recording or app network service. The verified Android build requests no permissions. It creates no child profiles. This describes the app, not data processing by Google Play, Android or other apps.

## Progress on this device

Points, completed tasks, language, character, clothes, scenery and audio/accessibility settings are saved in private app storage. There is no server save or synchronisation. The parents' reset action asks for confirmation and resets this data. Uninstalling removes private app storage. Android backup and device transfer of app data are disabled by the manifest and exclusion rules; practical device acceptance is still outstanding.

## Your own character image

An adult can select JPEG, PNG or static WebP in the parents area using Android's system selector. The app does not request broad photo, storage or camera access. It orients, reduces, optionally crops and re-encodes the image as PNG. Only selected pixels without EXIF, GPS or camera metadata are stored privately. The original file, its name and address are not retained. Cancelling or rejecting a file does not replace a saved image.

Kinderwelt does not upload, share or export the image. Cloud-backup and device-transfer exclusions concern the app copy. The unchanged original in a gallery, document provider or their own cloud remains outside the app's control. Only adults can add, replace or remove the app copy. Removal and confirmed reset delete it; uninstalling removes it with app data. Please do not send real child pictures or screenshots containing them in bug reports.

## Retention and contact

Local data stays on this device until removal, reset or uninstall. Kinderwelt sends no automatic crash reports.
