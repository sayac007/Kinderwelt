# Privacy Policy for the App “Kinderwelt”

Last updated: 26 October 2026

## 2. Summary

Kinderwelt processes game progress, settings and optional character pictures exclusively on the device and does not transmit this content to us or to third parties. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and requests no Android permissions. App backups to the cloud are disabled.

## 3. What is stored on the device

For the game to work, the app stores the following in its private storage area on the device:

- game progress, collected points and rewards
- settings such as language, selected character and game world
- optionally, a custom character picture (see section 4)

Other apps cannot access this data. It is not transmitted and not included in app backups. We as the provider have no access to it.

## 4. Custom character picture

Parents can select their own photo as a game character in the parents’ area. The following applies:

- The photo is selected via the operating system’s picker. The app only receives the selected picture and needs no permission to do so.
- After selection, a preview is prepared. Only after confirmation is a reduced-size copy stored, with metadata such as location and camera data removed. The original file remains unchanged in its original location.
- The copy is stored only in the app’s private storage area. It is not uploaded, not shared and not included in app backups.

## 5. Children

Kinderwelt is intended for primary school children. Game progress, settings and character pictures are processed exclusively on the device and are not transmitted to us or to third parties. Selecting a photo is restricted to the parents’ area.

## 6. Deleting data

- **Delete character picture:** possible at any time in the parents’ area.
- **Reset game progress** (parents’ area): deletes the character picture and resets game progress and settings to their defaults.
- **Uninstalling:** Android removes all of the app’s private data from the device.

The originally selected photo is not affected. It remains in your gallery or storage location and is never changed or deleted by Kinderwelt.

## 7. Google Play

The app is distributed via Google Play. When you download, install or update it, Google processes data under its own responsibility, for example information about your Google account and device. Depending on your device settings, Google may also collect technical diagnostic data such as crash reports and provide it to us in aggregated form. Kinderwelt itself contains no diagnostic or crash reporting tools. For details, see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 8. Contact by email

If you email us, for example with feedback or a bug report, we use your email address and the content of your message solely to handle your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not share this data and delete it once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Please do not send us photos of children; such emails will not be processed and will be permanently deleted immediately.

## 9. Your rights

Where we process personal data about you, i.e. when you contact us by email, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

## 10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed, we will update this privacy policy. The current version is always available at this address.
